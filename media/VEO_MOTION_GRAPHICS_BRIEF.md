These clips support the real product recording. They should never imitate the Warden dashboard or imply that generated imagery is product footage.

## Shared visual language

- Format: 16:9, 4K, 24 fps
- Palette: near-black graphite, Warden teal, restrained security gold
- Style: premium enterprise motion design, dimensional but minimal, physically plausible light
- Camera: deliberate, slow, readable movement with no handheld motion
- Typography: add titles during editing, not inside generated footage
- Continuity: use the same teal signal line and gold authority envelope in all three clips
